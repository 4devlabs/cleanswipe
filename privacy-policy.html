Privacy Policy

How CleanSwipe handles your data

No Personal Data Collection

CleanSwipe does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, photos, or videos to external servers. All operations are performed locally on your device.

Device Permissions

CleanSwipe requests access to your photos and videos only so that it can display them and allow you to permanently delete the items you choose. These permissions are used solely for this core functionality.

Local Processing Only

All media processing and deletions happen directly on your device using the Android MediaStore system. CleanSwipe does not upload your media to any server and does not use cloud storage.

Analytics & Tracking

CleanSwipe does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries that collect your personal data or track you across apps and websites.

Purchases & Payments

If you choose to purchase a premium subscription, payments are processed securely by Google Play. CleanSwipe does not receive or store your full payment details such as credit card numbers.

Your Control

You can revoke app permissions at any time from your device settings, and you can stop using CleanSwipe at any time by uninstalling the app.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer using the support options provided in the app.
